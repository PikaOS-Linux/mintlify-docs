A reader for DER (X.690), the encoding of certificates, keys and ECDSA signatures.
Elements are positions in the caller's bytes rather than slices, so parsed records can
be stored and copied without borrowing what they were read from.

Only DER is accepted: definite minimal lengths, low tag numbers, minimal integers,
booleans of 0x00 or 0xff, and bit strings whose unused bits are zero. Anything else,
and every length that runs past its container, is `error.Malformed`, never a panic,
since the bytes come from the network.

```otr
import std.crypto.asn1
```


## time

```otr
fn time(der: []u8, el: Element) Error!i64
```

A UTCTime (`YYMMDDHHMMSSZ`, years 1950 to 2049 as RFC 5280 reads them) or
GeneralizedTime (`YYYYMMDDHHMMSSZ`), as seconds since the Unix epoch. RFC 5280 allows
no other forms: no fractions, no offsets, seconds always present.
