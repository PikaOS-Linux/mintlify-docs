### Inflater.step

```otr
fn step(self: &mut Inflater, input: []u8, output: []mut u8, final_input: bool) c.Error!c.Progress
```

EOF is explicit. After done, repeated calls consume/write nothing. On error,
inspect progress then discard/reset state. No input/output borrow is retained.
