## Connection

`connection.Connection.init(interest)` connects with a `GlobalInterest` selection. Optional advertised globals are owned fields on the connection. Check protocol availability before creating dependent resources.

`fd`, `flush`, `dispatch`, `dispatch_pending`, and `roundtrip` integrate with your loop. Dispatch methods requiring time/I/O take `std.Io` explicitly. `dispatch_or` can wait for Wayland, one extra descriptor, or a deadline.

Consume `next_global()` records; the connection does not support callback registration.

## Hub and components

`hub.Hub` stores records from generated protocol listeners. Each tracker keeps a cursor and applies records during `pump`. Use a component's `next_event` or generation value after pumping to detect changes.

Store `wl.Id` for identity. Borrow a live proxy from its owning tracker when sending a request. An identity is not permission to reuse a destroyed proxy.

## Surfaces

- `layer`: layer, anchor, and keyboard-interactivity values.
- `xdg_toplevel`: reusable XDG window lifecycle and configure events.
- `layer_popup`: layer-shell popups.
- `background_effect`: compositor blur regions when supported.
- `dim_overlay`: shared dimming surfaces.

A compositor is external to Otter Shell. Handle unavailable optional protocols rather than assuming a particular compositor.

## Outputs and workspaces

`capture_output.OutputList` stores output state. `output_scale` reads exact scale information from it. Keep exact factors distinct from integer ceiling scale.

`foreign_toplevel`, `ext_workspace`, and `workspace_provider` expose window/workspace state. Compositor IPC helpers use explicit `ipc.Endpoints` and I/O, not environment singletons.
