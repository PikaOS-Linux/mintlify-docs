## SHM ownership

`otter_wayland.shm` owns shared-memory buffers and tracks compositor ownership. Acquisition returns a `Lent` value containing an owned render `Surface` and slot index, not an unbounded borrowed pixel slice.

Return the lent mapping through the pool's attach or unsubmitted-release path. Do not retain a surface after giving it back, reuse a compositor-owned buffer, or treat a release event as a global permission to rewrite all slots.

Pools process release/configure records through the hub. Retired mappings must remain alive until the compositor releases their buffers.

## Damage

`otter_wayland.damage.DamageTracker` accumulates dirty rectangles with double-buffer behavior and a full-redraw fallback when bounded storage overflows.

Use the same logical/physical coordinate conventions for emitted commands, render-culling damage, and Wayland submissions. Exact output scale is not interchangeable with its integer ceiling.

## Renderer

`otter_wayland.renderer.Renderer` is a CPU renderer stored by value. It has no heap-allocated vtable API.

Rasterization resolves image handles through an image source and borrows font/shaping resources during the call. Follow the pool's acquire/attach lifecycle around that work.

A move of already-rendered pixels may need submit damage without rendering those pixels again. Keep submit-only damage out of render-culling sets.
