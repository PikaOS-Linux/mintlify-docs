## Ownership and context

`DesktopEntry` stores one owned text blob plus spans. Use `text(span)`, iterators, or `clone(alloc)` instead of retaining pointers into another entry.

`AppList.scan(env)` receives environment explicitly. Directory-specific variants support isolated fixtures.

`IconResolver.init(env, alloc, theme)` owns resolution state. Theme/path caches are not permission to keep an image pointer across image-store mutations.

Launch functions take a reaper and environment. Keep the reaper in the event loop so launched children are collected. `LaunchOptions` borrows overrides and URI lists for the call.

## Lua

- `otter.desktop.apps`: `list`, `find`, `search`, `actions`, `subscribe`; `launch` requires `spawn`.
- `otter.desktop.xdg_open`: content-type and handler reads; `open`/`open_with` require `spawn`.
- `otter.desktop.icons`: lookup/theme operations with a plugin-owned resolver.

A listed desktop entry is not itself a grant to execute it.
