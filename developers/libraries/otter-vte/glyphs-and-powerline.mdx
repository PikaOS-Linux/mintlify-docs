## Custom glyph rectangles

`otter_vte.cell_glyphs.build` produces geometry for custom terminal glyphs. `GlyphRects` stores up to 16 rectangles and exposes `slice()`.

Fractional blocks, quadrants, and braille use `append_bottom_frac`, `append_quadrant`, and `append_braille`. This geometry remains separate from font fallback.

## Operation lists

`otter_vte.ops.PowerlineOps` and `BlockOps` initialize with an allocator, reserve capacity explicitly, and reuse storage through `reset`. `append` records operations; `items()` exposes the recorded slice. The public `dropped` field records overflow.

Payload types such as `PowerlineOp`, `PowerlineKind`, and `BlockOp` live at module level rather than nested under the list types.

## Rounded masks

`RoundMaskCache` is caller-owned, not a mutable module global. The terminal `Renderer` owns one and passes it to powerline drawing. Masks allocate lazily on first use.

## Lua

`otter.vte.glyph(cp, x, y, w, h)` returns rectangles plus alpha, or nil. `powerline(cp)` classifies a glyph.

Plugin canvases support `clear`, `draw_powerline`, `draw_glyph`, `pixel`, and `free`. At most four canvases belong to one plugin, each bounded to 2 Mi physical pixels. The host releases them when that plugin instance unloads.
